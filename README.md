## Projects

### 🦅 GARUDA — Cyber-Physical Threat Intelligence Platform
*iDEX · Ministry of Defence, Government of India*

A multi-layer threat detection and autonomous response platform for defence networks and critical national infrastructure. Built five integrated subsystems: hardware-level side-channel telemetry (CPU/DRAM power via Intel RAPL), kernel process provenance tracking (eBPF kprobes), active infrastructure hunting against Certificate Transparency logs, Bayesian adversary attribution modelling across all 14 MITRE ATT&CK tactics, and deterministic containment with DNS sinkholing and non-destructive process freezing.

`Python` `eBPF/BCC` `FastAPI` `React` `PostgreSQL` `STIX 2.1` `TAXII 2.1` `Docker`

→ [**View Repository**](https://github.com/singhharsimar23-dotcom/garuda)
